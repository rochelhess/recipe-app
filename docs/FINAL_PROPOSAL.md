## Final proposal: Recipe App

### The Problem

Recipes are scattered across bookmarks, screenshots, and paper cards. It's hard to find a recipe quickly, keep your own versions, or share them in one place.

### The Idea

A web app where people can create an account, store their own recipes, and browse recipes added by others. Each recipe has ingredients, step-by-step instructions, prep and cook times, servings, and an optional photo.

### Who It's For

- Home cooks who want one organized place for their recipes
- Families who want to keep and share favorite dishes

### Core Features

- Browse all recipes and search by title
- View a full recipe
- Register, log in, and log out
- Add, edit, and delete your own recipes (owner-only)
- Recipes saved in a database so they persist

### Possible Extra Features (stretch goals)

- Categories and tags (dessert, dinner, vegetarian)
- Favorites list
- Image uploads
- Scaling ingredient amounts by servings
- A "my recipes" page

### Planned Tech

- React and Next.js for the frontend and API routes
- MongoDB with Mongoose for the database
- JWT-based authentication
- Jest and React Testing Library for tests
- Render for deployment

## Why I Chose the Recipe App

I love to cook, but i hate opening 10 different recipe books to find the perfect recipe. One app with all my favorite recipes, and family members' favorite recipes is a dream come true for me!!.
